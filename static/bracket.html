Predict the full knockout bracket before the Round of 32 kicks off — pick the team that advances from every match.

Points per correct pick:

Round of 32 (R32)5 pts
Round of 16 (R16)5 pts

Semi-finals (SF)20 pts

Tiebreakers (in order):

The winner earns bragging rights. Nothing else to win — but that's the best prize anyway.
