📖 Rules
How the WC 2026 prediction game works

Predictions

Predict the final score of each match. You can change your prediction any time — but predictions lock 5 minutes before kickoff and cannot be edited after that.

Scores are based on regulation time (90'). If a knockout match goes to extra time, the score at the end of 120' is used. Penalty shootout goals do not count.

A draw prediction in a knockout match is valid — it means you're predicting the match goes to extra time / penalties.

A correct outcome means you predicted the right winner (or draw). An exact score earns the higher points instead — they don't stack.

Rarity Bonus

On top of exact score points, a rarity bonus applies if fewer than 5% of players predicted the same exact scoreline:

⭐ +5 pts for a rare exact score in the Group Stage
⭐ +10 pts for a rare exact score in any Knockout round

Rarity is calculated after kickoff using the final locked predictions — you can't game it by watching others.

Group Stage Standings

Live group tables update every time a result is entered. Top 2 from each group advance to the Round of 32, plus the 8 best 3rd-placed teams.

Tiebreaker order: Points → Goal Difference → Goals For.
